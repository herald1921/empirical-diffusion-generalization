SETUP AND EXACT IDENTITIES
The same radius-R circle (R=2), with N=16 equally spaced training sites and a uniform target, is embedded in R^D for D=2,4,8,16. The additional D-2 coordinates contain independent N(0,eta^2) noise. The intrinsic dimension remains one. Every coupling has the same additional orthogonal cost, so W2_D^2=W2_2^2+(D-2)eta^2 and WN_D^2=WN_2^2+(D-2)eta^2 exactly; WT and the empirical-Tweedie output law do not depend on D. All plotted errors are divided by the same S_N=0.22650649184.

DIVERSITY DEFICIT
For 0<=r<R, the projection of a sample ball covers a circle arc of angular half-width beta=arcsin(r/R). With alpha=pi/N and g=max(alpha-beta,0), the uniform target gives G(r)^2=(2R^2/alpha)(g-sin g). This follows by integrating squared chord distance to the nearest covered arc endpoint over the uncovered portions of a Voronoi cell. It vanishes exactly at r>=R sin(pi/N), within the defined domain r<R. Each displayed G curve uses the corresponding ambient dimension D and r=c_{D,0.05}eta; it is deliberately omitted for r>=R, where nearest-point projection is not uniquely defined throughout U_r and lies outside the stated reach-tube domain. Direct independent quadrature agrees with the closed formula to 6.1e-16 in squared distance.
